Corrections and contributions

Rae de Lune; editorial compilation · 24 October 2026

Markdown text

Corrections should identify the affected work or claim, provide a source link or dated record, and distinguish authorship, collaboration, quotation, and influence. Preserve historical source text; add an editorial note or a clearly labeled revised edition. Keep publication, revision, and recollection dates separate.

For additional texts or images, record their author/creator, source, publication date where known, version, and permission basis. A name in the acknowledgements does not imply endorsement of every work. An open practice does not replace the permissions applying to individual works.

Update catalog.json when adding a source, then rebuild the static pages and checksums. Do not edit generated HTML as the sole source of a correction.
