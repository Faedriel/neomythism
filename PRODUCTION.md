# Production and editorial method

Collected and maintained by Rae de Lune, known artistically as Faedriel, with AI assistance in Codex. Fae, the symbiont of Faedriel, narrates the master volume's introductory history. Rae's factual history is the reference account of the chronology. Both use the same evidence and distinguish recollection, institutional records, publication dates, and later revisions. Collected works retain their original authors, coauthors, and text credits.

This collection is the author-designated canonical reference edition of Rae de Lune / Faedriel’s Neomythism writings and account of its development. “Canonical” identifies the edition designated by Rae. Neomythism remains an open practice; individual works retain their authorship and contributor credits.

HTML was converted to Markdown with source text, emphasis, explicit line breaks, and preformatted passages preserved. Compiled headings and navigation were adjusted for reading. Two image embeds became links. The author-hosted Substack and website texts of *Neomythism as Postdigital Pedagogy* and *A Neomythist Attitude Toward Machine Consciousness* are included in full with permission confirmed by Rae on 25 October 2026. These selections apply to every public derivative, including the master and search data. The separate preservation archive retains the original retrieved texts and assets.

Rae de Lune and Faedriel name the same human; Faedriel is the artist name. Fae's narration credit does not replace individual essay or journal authorship. The AI acknowledgements describe contributions across formation and continuing development, without assigning every named model to 2025. Exact model names in those acknowledgements are supplied by Rae.

Edition 0.7 was prepared for public release on 24 October 2026. This collection date does not replace the original publication dates of its works or establish earlier dates for the development of particular ideas.

Edition 0.8 adds the complete August 2026 interview *Introducing Faedriel*, preserving its published wording, questions, credits, and artwork. The interview is an artist's account; its recollections and descriptions remain in that voice. That addition retained the existing history documents and collected source texts. It was published through an ordinary Git commit, retaining the published edition 0.7 in repository history.

A subsequent editorial update on 24 October 2026 records Rae’s clarified account of formal theorizing from 2019–2026 through artistic practice and academic research, condenses the source and attribution notes, and shows the IRB screenshot once in the complete volume, with links at later mentions. Original published essays and interview text retain their wording. Earlier published editions and their commit timestamps remain in repository history.

The 25 October 2026 full-text addition replaces the source-link placeholders for *Neomythism as Postdigital Pedagogy* and *A Neomythist Attitude Toward Machine Consciousness* with their preserved author-hosted texts, following Rae’s permission confirmation. Their original publication dates and previous editions remain recorded, and publication uses another ordinary commit.
