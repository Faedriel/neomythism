# Rights and publication scope

Neomythism is an open practice. Accurate attribution and the permissions attached to particular works remain part of its record. No blanket reuse licence is granted for this mixed collection.

## Texts and images

The author directs republication of the collected essays and website writing with their original credits. On 24 October 2026, the author confirmed the right or permission to republish the three Glitch-associated essays and confirmed that the remaining essay images, apart from the identified Veo demo and World of Warcraft images, are their own work or cleared for republication. Existing source notices remain in place. This permission to include material here does not assign its authorship or grant a new public reuse licence.

The complete interview [Introducing Faedriel](interviews/2026-08-12-introducing-faedriel.md), published by Hakan Öztunalı on Patreon on 12 August 2026, is included with its original questions, answers, and accompanying artwork. Rae confirmed permission to republish the complete piece on 24 October 2026 and identified the artwork as their own. Questions and original publication are credited to Hakan Öztunalı; answers and artwork are credited to Faedriel. The interview retains its own description of collaboration with Fae. This republication permission does not grant a new public reuse licence.

Rae de Lune's *Made and Real* is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The article-specific attribution and changes notice accompanies each edition. The PDF is unchanged. Follow any source notices concerning third-party material.

Two subscription journal works—*Neomythism as Postdigital Pedagogy* and *A neomythist attitude toward machine consciousness*—have bibliographic records and links here while the exact manuscript versions and applicable sharing terms are unresolved. Both their Substack and website editions are represented. This is not a determination that the works cannot be shared in an appropriate version. The complete retrieved texts remain in the separate local preservation archive.

The Google Veo demo frame and World of Warcraft image are linked to their original sources rather than rehosted. Captions and surrounding essay text are preserved. The dissertation includes its abstract and institutional record; its full text was not supplied or retrieved. The USD record lists download availability on 28 April 2028. The IRB image and PDF are included at the author's request, with their existing redactions.
