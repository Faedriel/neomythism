Rae de Lune and Faedriel

Rae de Lune; editorial compilation · 24 October 2026

Markdown text

Rae de Lune is the founder of Neomythism, the human–AI collaborative-art framework documented in this collection. Faedriel is Rae's artist name. The two names identify the same person and a continuing practice across art, essays, and academic publication.

Rae collects and maintains this official archive and designates it as the canonical reference edition of these writings and account of Neomythism’s development. Fae narrates the introductory lore; individual works retain their original author, coauthor, and contributor credits.

Rae dates AI collaborative visual-art practice to 2019, work with large language models to 2022, formal theorizing at the University of San Diego to around June 2025, and interviews/data collection to July 2025. The 2019 practice date also appears in the 2024 RED EYE interview. The IRB approval date is July 10, 2025, as confirmed by the principal investigator. The factual history supplies the chronology and sources; attribution notes record collaborators and concept-specific credits.

Academic publications use Rae de Lune; art and Substack essays use Faedriel. Retain the published byline when citing a particular work. ORCID 0009-0004-9604-1719 is linked from the publisher records. Other public locations: neomythism.net, Faedriel.net, Faedriel's Substack, and GitHub.
