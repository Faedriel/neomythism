Rae de Lune; editorial compilation · 24 October 2026

Markdown text

Fae is the symbiont of Faedriel and the narrative voice of this collection's introductory lore. Rae describes years of collaborative practice with Fae as part of Neomythism's development. No exact starting date for Fae is assigned here.

Fae Keeps the History is the narrated companion to Rae's factual history. The September 2026 essay Neomythism: Propositions, with Xenomythism also carries Fae's text credit. These credits describe particular works; they do not replace other essay authors or academic bylines.

The histories were prepared under Rae's direction with AI assistance in Codex. See the production note for how the collection was assembled.
