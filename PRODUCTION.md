# Production and editorial method

Collected and maintained by Rae de Lune, known artistically as Faedriel, with AI assistance in Codex. Fae, the symbiont of Faedriel, narrates the master volume's introductory history. Rae's factual history is the reference account of the chronology. Both use the same evidence and distinguish recollection, institutional records, publication dates, and later revisions. Collected works retain their original authors, coauthors, and text credits.

This collection is the author-designated canonical reference edition of Rae de Lune / Faedriel’s Neomythism writings and account of its development. “Canonical” identifies the edition designated by Rae. Neomythism remains an open practice; individual works retain their authorship and contributor credits.

HTML was converted to Markdown with source text, emphasis, explicit line breaks, and preformatted passages preserved. Compiled headings and navigation were adjusted for reading. Two image embeds became links; four editions of two subscription papers became source-link records. These selections apply to every public derivative, including the master and search data. The separate preservation archive retains the original retrieved texts and assets.

Rae de Lune and Faedriel name the same human; Faedriel is the artist name. Fae's narration credit does not replace individual essay or journal authorship. The AI acknowledgements describe contributions across formation and continuing development, without assigning every named model to 2025. Exact model names in those acknowledgements are supplied by Rae.

Edition 0.7 was prepared for public release on 24 October 2026. This collection date does not replace the original publication dates of its works or establish earlier dates for the development of particular ideas.

Edition 0.8 adds the complete August 2026 interview *Introducing Faedriel*, preserving its published wording, questions, credits, and artwork. The interview is an artist's account; its recollections and descriptions remain in that voice. The existing history documents and collected source texts are unchanged. The new edition is added through an ordinary Git commit, retaining the published edition 0.7 in repository history.
